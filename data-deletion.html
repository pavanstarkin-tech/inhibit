DATA DELETION

Data Deletion & Retention Policy

App: Inhibit (com.inhibit.user)

1. No Remote Data Stored

Inhibit operates completely on-device and does not operate any backend servers or cloud databases. There is no remote personal data, account, or analytics stored that requires a deletion request.

2. How to Delete All Local Data on Android

All app settings, configurations, and interception counter statistics reside locally on your device. You can completely erase all local data at any time:

  1. Open your phone's Settings app.
  2. Navigate to Apps > Inhibit.
  3. Tap Storage & Cache.
  4. Tap Clear Storage (or Clear Data).

Uninstalling the Inhibit app also permanently deletes 100% of the locally stored preferences.
