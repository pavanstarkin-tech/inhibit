HELP & SUPPORT

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Can I watch a single Reel or Short without being interrupted?

Yes! Inhibit allows you to watch the 1st Reel or YouTube Short in its entirety with zero interruptions. You can replay it, listen to audio, and read comments. Inhibit only intervenes when you swipe vertically to advance to the next reel, preventing accidental doomscrolling.

2. Can I still view and upload Instagram Stories?

Absolutely. Viewing stories, replying to stories, taking photos with the story camera, and uploading stories are 100% unrestricted and never blocked.

3. What happens when a friend sends me a Reel in Direct Messages?

When you open a sent reel from chat or direct messages, Inhibit recognizes the intentional single-video interaction and lets you watch the entire video smoothly.

4. Why does Inhibit require Accessibility Permission?

Android requires Accessibility Permission for client-side apps to detect on-screen view containers (like Reels ViewPager) in real-time. Inhibit uses this permission strictly to stop doomscrolling locally. We do NOT read passwords, keystrokes, messages, or send any data to external servers.

5. What is Intentional Post Mode?

Intentional Post Mode grants a 30-minute unrestricted creator window, allowing you to create, edit, and post content to Instagram and YouTube without any shielding interventions.

6. Does Inhibit collect any personal data?

No. Inhibit collects zero personal data, zero analytics, and zero device metrics. Everything runs entirely offline on your device.
