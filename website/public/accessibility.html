Google Play AccessibilityService Declaration Guide

Package Name: com.inhibit.user

Google Play Review Declaration Statements

1. Core Functionality Declaration
Inhibit uses the AccessibilityService API exclusively for its core functionality: Digital Wellbeing & Short-Video Doomscroll Intervention. The service detects when a user is actively scrolling through endless algorithmic short-video feeds (Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts) and deterministically intervenes by returning the user to their home feed.
2. Is this an Accessibility Tool?
No. android:isAccessibilityTool is NOT declared as true. Inhibit is a rule-based digital wellbeing productivity tool.
3. Prominent In-App Disclosure & Consent
Before navigating to Android Accessibility Settings, Inhibit displays an explicit, prominent in-app disclosure modal detailing exactly what views are evaluated and requiring affirmative user consent ("Accept and Continue").
4. Data Safety & Non-Collection
Zero data is collected, logged, or transmitted off the device. No personal information, keystrokes, messages, or credentials are read.
5. Review Demonstration Video Elements
Review video includes:
  • App launch and navigation to onboarding/shield screen.
  • Display of the Prominent Disclosure dialog.
  • Affirmative user consent and Android settings navigation.
  • Demonstration of Reel #1 playing uninterrupted and Reel #2 scroll interception smoothly returning to home feed.
