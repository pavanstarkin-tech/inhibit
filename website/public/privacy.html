Privacy Policy & Accessibility Service Disclosure

Effective Date: September 18, 2026

App Name: Inhibit (com.inhibit.user)

🔒 Zero-Data Collection Guarantee: Inhibit operates 100% on-device. We do NOT collect, store, transmit, log, track, or share ANY personal information, keystrokes, messages, browsing history, media, or credentials.

1. Introduction

Inhibit ("we", "us", or "our") is designed from the ground up to respect user privacy and promote digital wellbeing. Our core mission is to help users maintain healthy digital boundaries without tracking or recording their private interactions.

2. Prominent Disclosure: Android AccessibilityService Usage

Why Inhibit Uses the AccessibilityService API:

Inhibit utilizes Android's AccessibilityService API for the legitimate and primary core functionality of Digital Wellbeing and Doomscrolling Intervention.

The Accessibility Service allows the app to:

  • Detect in real-time when short-form video viewers (such as Instagram Reels or YouTube Shorts) are active.
  • Distinguish between single video watching (which is fully allowed) and continuous infinite vertical scrolling.
  • Perform a standard in-app back navigation (GLOBAL_ACTION_BACK) when infinite doomscrolling is detected, safely redirecting users back to their feed.

Google Play Accessibility Compliance Declarations:

  • No Accessibility Tool Claim: Inhibit does not claim isAccessibilityTool="true". It is a digital wellbeing automation tool operating on deterministic, user-configured rules.
  • No Keylogging or Text Logging: The service does NOT intercept, read, or record private chat messages, passwords, credit card numbers, or keyboard inputs.
  • No Remote Transmission: Zero accessibility events or window content are ever transmitted over the Internet. All UI evaluation runs in local volatile memory.
  • No Settings Tampering: Inhibit does not alter system settings or prevent users from uninstalling or disabling the service at any time.
  • Safe Surfaces 100% Unrestricted: Normal feed browsing, viewing stories, uploading stories, camera usage, and direct messages are never blocked or modified.

3. Information We Collect (Zero Data Collection)

Inhibit does NOT collect any data. Specifically:

  • No Personal Identifiers: No name, email address, phone number, or device IDs are requested.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: There are no third-party tracking SDKs, advertising networks, or analytics frameworks included in the app.
  • Local Statistics Only: Interception statistics (e.g. daily doomscroll count) are stored strictly in local Android SharedPreferences on your device and are never backed up to remote servers.

4. Data Deletion & Retention

Because Inhibit collects zero remote data, there is no remote user data stored on any server. Users can instantly clear local app statistics by clearing the app cache/storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the app. See our Data Deletion Policy for step-by-step instructions.

5. User Consent & Permission Revocation

Accessibility permission is requested only after showing an explicit in-app disclosure. Users may enable or disable Inhibit's accessibility service at any time in Android Settings > Accessibility > Inhibit.

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or our accessibility implementation, please open an issue or contact us via our GitHub repository at github.com/pavanstarkin-tech/inhibit.
